# ISO 27001 vs DORA vs NIS2

| Framework | Type | Scope |
|-----------|------|-------|
| ISO 27001 | Standard | Any organization |
| DORA | Regulation | Financial sector |
| NIS2 | Directive | Critical sectors |

## Key Takeaway
ISO 27001 provides a strong baseline. DORA and NIS2 add specific legal obligations.
