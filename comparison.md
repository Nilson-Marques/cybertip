

# ISO 27001 vs DORA vs NIS2

These three frameworks improve cybersecurity and operational resilience, but they have different purposes and legal effects.

| Framework | Type | Scope | Read the Guide |
|-----------|------|-------|----------------|
| ISO/IEC 27001 | International standard | Organizations of any type or size | [ISO 27001 Guide](./iso-27001.html) |
| DORA | EU regulation | Financial entities and relevant ICT providers | [DORA Guide](./dora.html) |
| NIS2 | EU directive | Essential and important entities in critical sectors | [NIS2 Guide](./nis2.html) |

## Key Takeaway

[ISO/IEC 27001](./iso-27001.html) provides a strong foundation for building an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

[DORA](./dora.html) and [NIS2](./nis2.html) introduce legal cybersecurity and resilience obligations for organizations within their respective scopes.

ISO 27001 certification can help an organization demonstrate good security practices, but it does not automatically prove complete compliance with DORA or NIS2.
